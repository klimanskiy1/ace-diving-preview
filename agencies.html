Ace Diving accreditation

Authorized at every level

Ace Diving is a fully accredited dive center with active store numbers for PADI, SSI, SDI, TDI, and ERDI. This means we are officially recognized and authorized by each agency to conduct training at all levels, from beginner to professional and technical diving.

Every year, we certify students from all walks of life, from first time divers and vacationers to experienced divers advancing their skills and professionals entering the industry. Our team is committed to creating a supportive, safe, and enjoyable learning environment for every student, regardless of their background or experience level.

Our reviews consistently reflect this commitment, highlighting our professionalism, attention to safety, and the personalized approach we bring to every course and trip.

Divers often mention the confidence they gain, the quality of instruction, and the overall experience both in and out of the water, which is something we take great pride in.

The agencies

PADI · SSI · SDI · TDI · ERDI

Store 28242

PADI (Professional Association of Diving Instructors)

PADI is the world’s largest and most widely recognized scuba training organization. It offers a structured system of courses from beginner to professional levels, emphasizing safety, skill development, and environmental awareness. PADI certifications are accepted worldwide, making it a popular choice for recreational divers and those pursuing professional careers in diving.

Store 810003

SSI (Scuba Schools International)

SSI provides internationally recognized scuba and freediving training programs with a strong focus on flexibility and digital learning. SSI courses emphasize hands-on experience, dive knowledge, and personalized instruction. Their certification system allows divers to track progress and continue skill development seamlessly across locations globally.

Store 1006029

SDI (Scuba Diving International)

SDI specializes in modern recreational scuba training with a focus on performance-based learning and the use of current technology. SDI integrates open water diving with advanced courses such as Nitrox and specialty dives, preparing divers for safe and confident underwater experiences.

Store 1006029

TDI (Technical Diving International)

TDI is a leading agency in technical diving education, covering advanced dives beyond recreational limits, including deep, wreck, cave, and mixed-gas diving. Their programs prioritize rigorous safety standards, planning, and advanced equipment use, catering to divers who want to explore challenging underwater environments.

Store 1006029

ERDI (Emergency Response Diving International)

ERDI focuses on training professional divers in public safety, rescue, and emergency response scenarios. Courses cover search and recovery, underwater rescue, and emergency management, combining diving skills with critical life-saving techniques.
