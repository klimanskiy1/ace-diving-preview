Discover Scuba Diving

Are you ready to dive into an exciting new adventure and discover the wonders of scuba diving? Our expert team is here to turn your underwater dreams into reality!

Book hereCompare with Open Water
About

Your first breath underwater

We’ll start by teaching you essential skills in the pool, ensuring you feel both comfortable and confident. Then, get ready for an unforgettable journey! Led by our experienced instructors, you’ll explore a breathtaking underwater world, swimming alongside vibrant tropical fish, and possibly even spotting stingrays or turtles.

You’ll enjoy 20-45 minutes underwater per dive, depending on your air consumption, with two thrilling dives scheduled for you. Gear up and get ready for an action-packed day with Ace Diving — where fun, excitement, and adventure await!

A dive instructor will be with you at all times.

Duration

One day, 9 AM to 5 PM

Our all-inclusive Discover Scuba Diving course begins at 9 AM with a morning session in the pool, where one of our expert instructors will guide you through essential scuba skills. After your first experience of breathing underwater, you'll be ready for the thrilling boat dives in the afternoon!

Make sure to arrive at the boat by 12 PM for the afternoon departure, where you'll set off for two exciting boat dives. After an unforgettable day of underwater exploration, we'll return to the pier by 5 PM.

What to bring

Pack light

Don’t forget to bring a towel, biodegradable sunscreen, and your bathing suit for a comfortable and eco-friendly dive experience. Feel free to pack any refreshments you'd like to enjoy during the day, and we'll happily store them in our ice box to keep them cool. Whether it's a snack or a drink, we've got you covered!

Provided

Training & dive equipment

  • Ice cold water
  • Mask, snorkel, and fins
  • Buoyancy Control Device (BCD)
  • Regulator with alternate air source
  • Depth gauge
  • Underwater Compass
  • 2 Air Tanks and weights
  • Wetsuit if needed
