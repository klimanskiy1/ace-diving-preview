Dive deeper. Dive smarter.

The widest range of scuba certifications in Miami

Ready to dive in? Ace Diving Miami is your gateway to unforgettable underwater adventures. As the only local dive center authorized by PADI, SSI, SDI, TDI, and ERDI, we offer the widest range of scuba certifications from beginner courses to advanced specialties and Public Safety Diver training.

Our expert instructors make every dive safe, thrilling, and truly memorable. Whether you are exploring with Discover Scuba, snorkeling, or enjoying a private dive charter, the ocean adventure of your dreams is waiting.

We also provide Emergency First Response and First Response Provider training so you are confident and prepared on land and underwater.

Dive deeper. Dive smarter. Dive Miami with Ace Diving. 🌊🤿

Reefline Miami
