Rebreather

Rebreather training teaches experienced divers to safely use closed-circuit rebreathers (CCR), which recycle exhaled gas and enable longer quieter dives. Courses focus on rebreather mechanics, oxygen management, and emergency procedures, featuring both confined-water drills and open-water dives. The training is unit-specific and requires constant attention, often making seasoned divers feel like beginners.
