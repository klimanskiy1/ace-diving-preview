Private Charter

Welcome to Ace Diving, the premier provider of scuba diving and snorkeling services in Miami. Our Newton 46' Dive Special, M/V E-ZEN-TIAL, is perfect for exploring the beautiful coral reefs, wrecks or simply touring the beautiful landscapes of Miami.

Call to reserve · +1 (786) 241 - 3194

With our team of certified and experienced professionals, you can rest assured that you are in safe hands. We offer quality and personalized service, providing each of our customers with a memorable and enjoyable experience. Come aboard and let us take you on a journey like no other.

We take pride in being one of the most trusted scuba diving operators around. Our boat is specifically designed for scuba diving and snorkeling, featuring ample space and maximum diving comfort. We have a capacity for 49 passengers or 36 divers, so you won't have to worry about overcrowding either onboard or underwater. Book your next dive with Ace Diving and let us show you what we're all about.

Equipment rental

We understand that having the right diving equipment is crucial for a successful dive. That's why we offer brand new setups of Aqualung BCD's, regulators, wetsuits, fins, masks, snorkels, and Catalina 80 and 63 cf Aluminum tanks with air or 28% EAN for rent.

Our diving equipment is regularly serviced and checked to ensure reliability and safety. Let us provide you the perfect equipment for your next dive adventure.

Cancellation policy. We prioritize the safety and satisfaction of our customers above everything else. We understand that sometimes unexpected events happen, which is why we offer a flexible cancellation policy. Customers who provide us with a notice of cancellation at least 21 days prior to their scheduled trip will receive a full refund or credit. In addition, customers will also receive a full refund or credit in the unlikely event of operator cancellation due to weather or unforeseen circumstances prior to departure. No-shows will be charged the full price.
