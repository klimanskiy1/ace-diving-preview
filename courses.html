Basic Freediving

Includes e-learning, pool session and open water dives.

Freediving is a unique experience that focuses on breath control, relaxation, and moving effortlessly underwater without the use of tanks. In a freediving class, you’ll learn proper breathing techniques, safety procedures, and how to increase your breath hold while staying calm and in control. It’s not just about diving deeper, but about connecting with the ocean in a more natural and peaceful way, making it perfect for anyone looking to explore the underwater world on a single breath.

Dive Master

SDI, SSI or PADI Professional Course

Embarking on a professional diving career begins with completing a Divemaster course, provides comprehensive knowledge of all diving aspects. You'll be trained to take responsibility for fellow divers, guide them on their dives, and assist in various courses.

At Ace, we provide comprehensive training that goes beyond the basic requirements. You will spend extensive time on the boat gaining hands-on training, real-world experience, and valuable industry knowledge. Throughout the process, you will learn practical tips and tricks from experienced professionals in the field, helping you develop both technical skills and confidence on the job.

In addition, you will have the opportunity to earn your Compressor Technician Certification, further strengthening your qualifications and preparing you for long-term success in the marine and diving industry.

Referral

SDI, SSI or PADI Course, 4 boat dives — includes all equipment for training & certification

The Referral System gives you a variety of options to complete your diving education. After completing the Academic training and Confined Water training portions of the your Open Water Diver Course somewhere else in the world, we can finish the course and provide you with an SDI, SSI or PADI Open Water Certification.

If you have completed the academic and confined water training with a certifying agency other than SDI, SSI or PADI, we are generally able to convert your training and issue you with an SDI, SSI or PADI Open Water Diver Certification.
