Customer Policies

Book with confidence. Our policies are designed to be fair to our customers while allowing us to properly prepare our vessel, crew, instructors, guides, tanks, and equipment for every scheduled trip.

Full booking policies
Quick reference

Know before you book

Tip

The crew works hard, before, during and after each trip to insure that you have a fun, safe and clean experience aboard our vessel. It is customary on a dive boat to tip the crew $25 per person or if you are a booking of 6+ passengers to tip 20% of your booking. We accept VENMO @AceDivingMiami, CASHAPP $AceDivingMiami, ZELLE (info@ace-diving.com) and PAYPAL (Carlos Pineda). Your generosity is thanked!

Cancellations & Refunds
  • Cancel 48+ hours before departure → Full refund or credit.
  • Cancel within 48 hours or no-show → Full charge.
  • If we cancel before departure (unsafe weather, mechanical issues, or unforeseen events) → Full refund or credit.
  • Trips leave on time. Please arrive 60 minutes early to park, check in, and board. Missed departures = no-show.

Please note that once a trip is rescheduled by the customer, the option to cancel and obtain a refund is no longer available. In that case, the only available option is to receive a gift certificate.

Snorkelers
  • Sites are selected for snorkeling, but the ocean is unpredictable.
  • If the trip is called off after the first snorkel stop, no refunds will be issued.
  • Snorkelers must know how to swim comfortably.
Divers
  • Proof of certification is required. No proof = no diving, no refund.
  • Nitrox divers must show certification. Not yet certified? Ask us about our Nitrox course.
  • If the trip is called off after the first dive stop, no refunds will be issued.
  • For safety, divers must follow standard no-fly rules (minimum 18 hours before air travel).
Courses & Discover Scuba Diving
  • eLearning, pool, or ocean training already started = no refund.
  • Uncompleted courses may be rescheduled within 6 months, subject to availability and weather. Rescheduling fee applies.
  • Discover Scuba Diving experiences not completed due to personal reasons are non-refundable.
  • Failure to complete required eLearning before scheduled training = no refund and rescheduling fee applies.
Gear & Equipment
  • Tanks and weights are included with your dive trip.
  • Full gear rentals are available; please reserve in advance.
  • Guests are responsible for all gear provided to them. Lost or damaged gear must be paid for in full to ACE DIVING.
  • You are responsible for checking and securing your personal gear.
Health & Safety
  • Guests must be in good physical health to participate. If you have medical conditions, please bring a signed doctor’s clearance if required.
  • Arriving under the influence of drugs or alcohol → no boarding, no refund.
  • Non-swimmers may join as bubble watchers (boat riders only), but cannot enter the water. No refund will be issued if attempted.
  • The Captain and crew may end any trip or activity if they determine it is unsafe.
Vessel Rules
  • No firearms on board. ACE DIVING reserves the right to refuse boarding if a firearm is present.
  • Smoking & vaping are strictly prohibited.
  • Children must be supervised by a parent/guardian at all times.
  • Respect the crew, vessel, and fellow guests — unsafe or disruptive behavior will not be tolerated.
Environmental Responsibility
  • Please use reef-safe sunscreen and avoid touching coral or marine life.
  • Do not remove shells, plants, or animals from the ocean.
  • Help us keep the ocean clean — no trash overboard.
What to Bring
  • Towel, reef-safe sunscreen, reusable water bottle, and a positive attitude!
  • Light snacks and drinks are welcome, but please keep it tidy.
