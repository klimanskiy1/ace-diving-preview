Trips & Experiences

Real ocean diving. Real people. Real fun. Daily scuba and snorkel trips out of Downtown Miami to reefs, wrecks, ledges and the Neptune Memorial Reef.

Book a tripDive sites

At Ace Diving, it’s not just about getting in the water. It’s about the full experience from the moment you step on the boat to the last high five back at the dock.

We run daily scuba and snorkel trips out of Downtown Miami, exploring some of the best dive sites in South Florida. Most of our standard trips are two tank dives, giving certified divers the chance to explore reefs, wrecks, and ledges that are full of life. Expect sea turtles, nurse sharks, massive schools of fish, moray eels, and if you’re lucky, the occasional eagle ray cruising by.

One of our signature weekday stops is the Neptune Memorial Reef, the world’s first underwater cemetery sitting in about 40 feet of water. It’s a surreal and beautiful dive, especially for photography. We usually pair that with a natural reef dive in the 15 to 30 foot range, perfect for relaxed bottom time and spotting marine life.

For those looking for something different, we also run trips to Biscayne National Park, where divers and snorkelers explore shallow coral reefs and historic shipwrecks inside the park boundaries. It’s a great option for families or mixed groups since the reefs are shallow and colorful, and the water is typically calm.

Not certified yet? No problem.

Our Discover Scuba Diving experience is perfect for first timers. After a short training session with one of our instructors, you’ll head out on the boat and complete two real ocean dives under direct supervision. It’s hands on, safe, and a lot more exciting than just staying in the pool.

If you prefer to stay on the surface, our snorkel trips are just as memorable. Clear water, vibrant reefs, and plenty of marine life without needing certification. We also welcome bubble watchers who want to ride along and enjoy the day on the water without getting in.

And for the adventurous crowd, we host specialty dives, lionfish hunts, lobster trips, advanced and tech training, and even professional level courses. Whether you’re working on your first certification or going after Divemaster, you’re diving with a team that teaches PADI, SSI, SDI, and ERDI courses.

The boat itself is a 46 foot Newton dive special built for comfort and space, designed specifically for divers. Plenty of room, easy entries, and a crew that keeps things organized and fun.

At the end of the day, what really makes the experience stand out is the vibe. We keep it professional, safe, and organized, but always relaxed. Divers of all levels feel welcome here, whether it’s your first ocean dive or your hundredth.

That’s what trips with Ace Diving are all about. Real ocean diving. Real people. Real fun.
